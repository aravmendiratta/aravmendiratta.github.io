A little more about my journey, work & life.

I'm currently pursuing my M.Sc. in Computer Science at the University of Freiburg, focusing on Artificial Intelligence, Bioinformatics, and Distributed Systems, with plans to complete my master's in 2027. Before moving to Germany, I completed my B.Tech in Computer Science & Engineering at SRM Institute of Science & Technology. My work sits at the intersection of generative AI, high-throughput computational biology, and AI-native business transformation. I always position myself as domain-agnostic. I focus on results, pragmatic problem-solving, and efficient working across any challenge.

I have been fortunate to have both industry and research experience while working. On the industry side with Capgemini, the first half of my internship was in a consulting role within the Insights & Data team for business transformations. I worked with success stories from across Capgemini to build compelling business proposals, speaking directly with clients from various industries to understand their needs and converting complex technical offerings into clear business insights. For the second half of the internship, I transitioned into a GenAI developer role with the AUDI team. I worked alongside designers from AUDI to build a tool that helps them significantly reduce their manual design and mockup phase, taking on a backend role dealing with AWS, Docker, and cloud services.

On the research side at CRC 1597 'Small Data', we wanted to explore using LLMs as relational judges to construct ordinal embeddings, dealing with high-dimensional single-cell genomic data. My focus was hands-on engineering: building end-to-end LLMOps pipelines, implementing guardrails, and running extensive prompt optimization for reliable biological representation learning.

At work, I am fundamentally a team player who values feedback above everything else. I believe in listening first, communicating transparently, and holding myself to high personal standards. Being on time everywhere and respecting everyone's time is something I live by.
